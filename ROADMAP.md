# Player experience rollout

## Stage 1 — recovery, readability and crossings

Free Tour recovery (+4 seconds), optional Hardcore records and medals, instant retry, stepping drums, overhead hammer models, hazard warning visuals, next-flag distance, responsive lobby and a Game Over badge.

Also shipped: closed mountain meshes, checkpoint camera recovery, late-level bot survival regressions, mode-specific instructions, distance-based Endurance earnings with capped revival pricing, and clean-run checks that include impacts and recoveries.

## Stage 2 — learning and personal progress

Interactive movement tutorial; jump buffering; personal best ghosts; short stages and celebrations; assistance after repeated failures; live medal pace; tune finale transitions from measured player runs. Add a true buffered death replay (the current impact scene shows the aftermath).

## Stage 3 — rewards and replay

Daily visit and challenge rewards, larger first-clear rewards, a free revival allowance, and finish share cards. The existing clean-run XP bonus, distance-based Endurance earnings and gentler capped revival prices are already implemented.

## Stage 4 — friends and competition

Weekly seeded challenge, server-verified leaderboard, friend ghost codes, spectating and a next-race queue. Preserve cosmetics-only competitive multiplayer.

## Stage 5 — accessibility and device support

Colourblind palettes, reduced motion, remappable keys, gamepad input and a playable 2D fallback. Retain automatic quality adjustment, touch controls and solo auto-pause.

Each stage requires functional checks and desktop/mobile play checks before release. Stages 2–5 are planned except for the explicitly noted existing features; they are not claims about shipped functionality.
